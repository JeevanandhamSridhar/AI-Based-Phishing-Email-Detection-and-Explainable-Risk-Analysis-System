## 2. Key Technical Justifications

### Q1: Why not just use an LLM API (like GPT-4) for the entire pipeline?
**Answer:**
1. **Defensive Privacy & Data Protection:** In a corporate SOC, sending raw internal communications or executive emails to third-party cloud APIs poses serious compliance and data leakage risks.
2. **Cost & Latency Constraints:** Third-party APIs incur recurring costs per token and network latency. Our system runs locally at zero recurring cost ($0 software budget) with sub-second inference.
3. **Determinism & Reproducibility:** LLM outputs can hallucinate and drift. A calibrated local pipeline (TF-IDF + Logistic Regression + Static Heuristics) is fully auditable and deterministic.

### Q2: Why is the baseline model TF-IDF + Logistic Regression instead of a Deep Transformer?
**Answer:**
1. **Explainability Precision:** In linear models, SHAP values are exact and mathematically provable without Monte Carlo approximations.
2. **Resource Efficiency:** Logistic Regression runs instantly on any standard laptop without requiring dedicated GPU acceleration.
3. **Overfitting Prevention:** Given typical email body lengths, sublinear TF-IDF with L2 regularization provides robust generalization without memory bloat.

### Q3: Why are AI-Authorship and Adversarial Robustness reported separately rather than blended into the 100-point risk score?
**Answer:**
They answer fundamentally different questions:
- The 100-point **Risk Score** answers: *"How dangerous is this email to the organization?"*
- **AI-Authorship** answers: *"Was this communication likely generated by an LLM or written by a human?"* (An email can be legitimate and AI-assisted, or malicious and human-crafted).
- **Adversarial Robustness** answers: *"How brittle is our detection model against targeted evasion?"*
Blending them would distort the primary risk triage metric.

### Q4: How do you defend against claims of novelty? Are you claiming "first ever"?
**Answer:**
We explicitly **do not** claim "first ever." As documented in our literature review (`docs/research-gap.md`), individual techniques like heuristic parsing, ML classification, and SHAP explanations are well-established. Our contribution is the **first-class integration of a local-first, zero-cost architecture combining explainable multi-signal scoring with an AI-authorship detector and an explanation-guided adversarial self-evaluation that honestly reports its own failure modes**.

### Q5: How is safety guaranteed during analysis?
**Answer:**
All analysis is strictly static:
- **No URL Fetching:** Links are parsed solely as text strings to analyze syntax, entropy, and domain typosquatting; no HTTP/DNS requests are made.
- **No Attachment Execution:** Attachments are evaluated via header metadata, double-extension patterns, MIME verification, and SHA-256 hashing without being written to disk or executed.
